This Week in EJ

This week at a glance

This week's stories span all five boroughs and reflect the breadth of environmental justice challenges facing New York City communities. In the Bronx, residents are pushing back against diesel truck pollution near Hunts Point while a separate deal moves three aging peaker plants closer to retirement. Brooklyn's Red Hook neighborhood remains exposed ahead of hurricane season as its long-promised flood barrier stalls, and federal regulators are pressing for faster action on the Gowanus Canal Superfund cleanup. Meanwhile, a proposed data center in West Harlem is drawing fierce opposition from environmental advocates, and Queens residents turned out in force to contest a waste transfer station permit renewal in Maspeth.
